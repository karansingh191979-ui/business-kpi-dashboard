1. Project & Dataset Overview

This interactive dashboard analyzes customer retention and churn dynamics using the official customer_churn_sample.csv dataset. The data contains 15 verified records encompassing demographic attributes, account tenure, financial metrics, contract types, payment methods, support ticket history, and churn status.

2. Data Cleaning & Validation Rules

  • Duplicate Check: Checked CustomerID column. 0 duplicates found (15 unique records: CUST-1001 to CUST-1015).
  • Missing Values: Inspected numerical and categorical columns. 0 missing or null values present.
  • Data Types: Verified Tenure Months (Integer), Monthly Charges (Float), Total Charges (Float), Support Tickets (Integer).
  • Consistency Verification: Re-computed Total Charges against Tenure × Monthly Charges. All values strictly align.

3. KPI Definitions & Formulas

Total Customers: Count of unique customer records ($N_{total} = 15$).
Churned Customers: Count where Churn Label == "Churned" ($N_{churn} = 7$).
Churn Rate (%): (Churned Customers / Total Customers) × 100 = (7 / 15) × 100 = 46.67%.
Retention Rate (%): (Retained Customers / Total Customers) × 100 = (8 / 15) × 100 = 53.33%.
Revenue at Risk (Monthly MRR): Sum of Monthly Charges for all churned customers = $49.99 + $49.99 + $79.99 + $49.99 + $49.99 + $79.99 + $49.99 = $409.93 / month.
Average Customer Tenure: Sum of tenure values / Total customers = 282 / 15 = 18.80 Months.
